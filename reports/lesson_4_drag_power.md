Aerodynamics

Learned that aerodynamic power is the rate of energy required to overcome aerodynamic drag.
Learned that drag power increases with the cube of vehicle speed.
Learned that aerodynamic power depends linearly on drag coefficient and frontal area.
Understood why small increases in speed can dramatically increase energy consumption.

Applications

Drag power can be used to estimate motor power required to maintain a constant speed.
It allows estimation of aerodynamic energy losses during a race.
It forms the basis for energy consumption simulations and vehicle optimization.
It will be used later in Pulse & Glide optimization and AI driving strategy.

Key Takeaway
Aerodynamic power grows with the cube of velocity.
A small increase in speed can result in a much larger increase in required power, making speed optimization critical in energy-efficient vehicles such as Eco Shell Marathon prototypes.
