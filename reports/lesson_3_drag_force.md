Aerodynamics

Learned that aerodynamic drag depends on dynamic pressure, drag coefficient and frontal area.
Learned that drag force increases with the square of velocity.
Learned the importance of the CdA value when comparing vehicle aerodynamic performance.

Application

Drag force calculation allows estimation of aerodynamic energy losses.
The model can be used to compare different vehicle body designs.
Drag force is a key parameter for estimating required motor power and energy consumption.
